Although the committee’s report was praised for its thoroughness, critics noted that its conclusions were oddly , hedging on nearly every recommendation it made.

Неверно. Верный ответ — A, equivocal

Разбор

1. Подсказка — «hedging on nearly every recommendation»: выводы уклончивы. Это и есть equivocal — двусмысленный, уклончивый.

2. Ваш ответ — exhaustive («исчерпывающий»). Соблазняет, потому что рядом стоит thoroughness. Но although требует контраста с похвалой, а не её повтора.

1. The clue is “hedging on nearly every recommendation”: the conclusions avoided commitment. That is what equivocal means — deliberately ambiguous.

2. Your answer, exhaustive (“covering everything”), is tempting because thoroughness appears nearby. But although signals a contrast with the praise, not a repeat of it.
