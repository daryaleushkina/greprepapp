Text Completion

Although the committee’s report was praised for its thoroughness, critics noted that its conclusions were oddly equivocal, hedging on nearly every recommendation it made.

  • (A) equivocal верный ответ
  • (B) incisive
  • (C) exhaustive ваш ответ
  • (D) dogmatic
  • (E) lucid
!

Неверно. Верный ответ — A, equivocal

Подсказка — „hedging on nearly every recommendation“: выводы уклончивы. Это и есть equivocal — двусмысленный, уклончивый.

Ваш ответ — exhaustive („исчерпывающий“). Соблазняет, потому что рядом стоит thoroughness. Но although требует контраста с похвалой, а не её повтора.

The clue is “hedging on nearly every recommendation”: the conclusions avoided commitment. That is what equivocal means — deliberately ambiguous.

Your answer, exhaustive (“covering everything”), is tempting because thoroughness appears nearby. But although signals a contrast with the praise, not a repeat of it.
