Although the committee’s report was praised for its thoroughness, critics noted that its conclusions were oddly , hedging on nearly every recommendation it made.

  • A equivocal верный
  • C exhaustive ваш ответ
  • B incisive D dogmatic E lucid

Неверно. Верный ответ — A, equivocal. Incorrect. The answer is A, equivocal.

Подсказка — «hedging on nearly every recommendation»: выводы уклончивы. Это и есть equivocal — двусмысленный, уклончивый.

The clue is “hedging on nearly every recommendation”: the conclusions avoided commitment. That is what equivocal means — deliberately ambiguous.

exhaustive — «исчерпывающий». Соблазняет, потому что рядом стоит thoroughness. Но although требует контраста с похвалой, а не её повтора.

exhaustive (“covering everything”) is tempting because thoroughness appears nearby. But although signals a contrast with the praise, not a repeat of it.
