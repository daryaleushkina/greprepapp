Although the committee’s report was praised for its thoroughness, critics noted that its conclusions were oddly , hedging on nearly every recommendation it made.

  1. A equivocal Верный ответ
  2. B incisive
  3. C exhaustive Ваш ответ
  4. D dogmatic
  5. E lucid

Неверно. Верный ответ — A, equivocal

AПодсказка — „hedging on nearly every recommendation“: выводы уклончивы. Это и есть equivocal — двусмысленный, уклончивый.

CВаш ответ — exhaustive („исчерпывающий“). Соблазняет, потому что рядом стоит thoroughness. Но although требует контраста с похвалой, а не её повтора.

AThe clue is “hedging on nearly every recommendation”: the conclusions avoided commitment. That is what equivocal means — deliberately ambiguous.

CYour answer, exhaustive (“covering everything”), is tempting because thoroughness appears nearby. But although signals a contrast with the praise, not a repeat of it.
